MSSP Quality Reporting 2026: what ACOs need to know

The measure set, the pass line, and the decisions that matter before the year closes.

Blog · Last checked November 17, 2026

← Blog

The short version

Shared Savings Program ACOs report quality through the APM Performance Pathway using the APP Plus measure set. The ACO reports clinical measures from its own data, a certified vendor runs the patient survey, and CMS calculates the claims-based measures. The result feeds the quality performance standard that decides how much of any savings the ACO keeps, or how much of any loss it owes.

How it is scored

  • Each measure is scored against a benchmark. Historical benchmarks apply to most eCQMs and MIPS CQMs; Medicare CQMs use flat benchmarks, and CMS finalized applying them retroactively to Quality IDs 001, 134 and 236 for 2026.
  • For PY 2026 the quality performance standard is a composite score of at least 73.85, which CMS derived from the 40th percentile of MIPS quality performance category scores.
  • An alternative standard is open to every ACO: a score at or above the 10th percentile on at least one of the four outcome measures. Shared savings and loss rates are scaled under it.
  • The eCQM/MIPS CQM reporting incentive is available only if you report all five clinical measures, meet data completeness on each, and avoid Medicare CQMs. ACOs that report any measure as a Medicare CQM are not eligible.
Check the detailsThe incentive and alternative-standard criteria above come from a third-party summary of CMS rules. Read them against CMS's own documents before you choose a collection type.

Decisions to make now

  1. Decide the collection type for each measure and confirm every participating practice can support it.
  2. Check 75% data completeness for every measure on data through the latest month.
  3. Compare your current rates with the posted benchmarks, measure by measure.
  4. Test your submission file before the window opens.
  5. Confirm your CAHPS survey vendor authorization is complete.

Data submission for the 2026 performance period is expected to run from January 4 to March 31, 2027, and data cannot be corrected after the window closes.

At a glance

  • ACOs report the APP Plus measure set
  • Five clinical measures, CAHPS for MIPS and claims measures
  • PY 2026 quality performance standard: composite of at least 73.85
  • Submission opens after the year ends and closes March 31
