MSSP Quality Calendar 2026: month by month

A working calendar for the 2026 performance year and the submission window that follows.

Blog · Last checked November 17, 2026

← Blog

Dates below come from CMS materials for the Quality Payment Program. The ACO-specific dates can change, so confirm each on qpp.cms.gov.

WhenWhatWho
January 2026Review the posted 2026 benchmarks. Lock measure versions. Start monthly completeness checks.Quality lead
February to April 2026Settle collection type per measure. Test extracts from every practice.Quality lead, IT
Summer 2026Confirm CAHPS for MIPS survey vendor authorization. CMS emails instructions in summer.ACO leadership
July to SeptemberMidyear review: compare rates with benchmarks and rerun the completeness check.Quality committee
October 2026Freeze the collection plan. Run a full validation against source systems.Quality lead
December 31, 2026Performance period ends. Collect last records.Practices
January 4, 2027 (expected)Submission window opens. Upload a test file, then the real one.Submitter
March 31, 2027, 8 p.m. ET (expected)Submission window closes. No corrections after.Submitter
Summer 2027Review final scores. Targeted review is available for about 60 days after release.ACO leadership
Build in slackSet your internal cutoff at least two weeks before March 31. Problems show up at upload time, not before.

Self-nomination for registries is a separate annual calendar. For 2026 it ran from July 1 to September 2, 2025. Registry teams should confirm the 2027 dates on the QPP site.

At a glance

  • Performance period: January 1 to December 31, 2026
  • Submission window expected: January 4 to March 31, 2027
  • Data cannot be corrected after the window closes
  • Final scores arrive in summer 2027
