Short, plain-language summaries for ACO quality, finance and data teams. Each item links to the primary source. Program rules change, so confirm against CMS before acting.

Analysis · Nov 13, 2026

CMS Finalizes Major Changes Designed to Grow the Medicare Shared Savings Program

A Bass, Berry & Sims summary of the CY 2027 final rule. BASIC Level E sharing rate rises from 50% to 60%, the prior savings adjustment scaling factor from 50% to 75%, and a new growth adjustment rewards ACOs that recruit providers. It also adds a Medicare eCQM collection type, replaces the CEHRT requirement with three options, and discontinues prepaid shared savings. Most financial changes apply to agreements starting January 1, 2027.

Bass Berry analysis → Final rule · Nov 4, 2026

CY 2027 Physician Fee Schedule final rule changes the Shared Savings Program

CMS issued CMS-1848-F on November 4, 2026. It changes the program's benchmarking and financial methodology to strengthen incentives for ACOs to participate. Most changes take effect January 1, 2027.

CMS fact sheet → Results · PY 2025 · Nov 17, 2026

Shared Savings Program saved Medicare more than $2.5 billion in 2025

CMS reports 472 ACOs, with 80% earning performance payments totaling $4.9 billion. Thirteen ACOs owed $23 million in shared losses. Net savings per beneficiary were $234, down from $241, which CMS links partly to more ACOs moving into two-sided risk. All ACOs met quality reporting requirements, and 264 reported at least one Medicare CQM, up from 64.

CMS fact sheet → Deadline · Nov 17, 2026

Phase 2 application window for a January 1, 2027 start closed at noon ET

The Phase 2 window ran November 12 through November 17, 2026. It was the last chance to correct Phase 2 deficiencies or modify Phase 2 responses. Phase 1 information, including the participant list, could not be changed in Phase 2.

CMS application timeline → Payment · 2027

New modifier B2 raises ACO office visit payment by 32%

Under the final rule, MSSP ACO participants and professionals, plus LEAD participant providers and ACO PC Flex participants, can use modifier B2. A law-firm analysis describes the payment as 32%. Read the CMS rule text for eligibility and billing details.

Law-firm analysis → Finance · PY 2025

PY 2025 reconciliation was delayed to apply the new benchmark policy

CMS delayed Performance Year 2025 reconciliation to November 2026 so it could apply a new lower limit. Analysts report the limit applies retroactively to ACOs that started in 2024, 2025 or 2026.

Lexology analysis → Risk · 2027

Legacy TINs will not count toward experience with risk

Starting in 2027, an ACO participant TIN without a written agreement to participate in a performance-based risk Medicare ACO initiative will not count as having participated. Legacy TINs in CMS Innovation Center ACO models will not count toward a Shared Savings Program ACO's experience with risk.

Chief Healthcare Executive → Assignment · 2028

Primary care billed through non-ACO TINs will stop counting for plurality

Starting in 2028, CMS will exclude primary care charges billed through a non-ACO TIN by ACO professionals when determining which ACO has the plurality of a beneficiary's primary care. Practices that bill through more than one TIN should review how this affects assignment.

Lexology analysis → Participation · 2026

Shared Savings Program grew to 511 ACOs for 2026

CMS approved 134 applications for the 2026 performance year, including 72 new ACOs and 62 renewing or reentering ACOs. Participation rose from 476 ACOs in 2025.

Becker's Payer → Results · PY 2024

Prior year: $4.1 billion in shared savings, $2.5 billion saved

In Performance Year 2024, Shared Savings Program ACOs earned shared savings totaling $4.1 billion and saved Medicare $2.5 billion, according to CMS. The full file is on Data.CMS.gov.
