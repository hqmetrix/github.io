Send ahead of the meeting

  • The scorecard, with each measure's rate, target, and change since last month.
  • A short list of data issues found since the last meeting.
  • The deep-dive material: one page on the measure, the population and the open question.
  • Last meeting's action log.

Standing scorecard columns

  • Measure and direction (higher or lower is better)
  • Numerator, denominator and rate
  • Target and benchmark
  • Change since last month and since last year
  • Status: on track, watch or gap
  • Open gaps and gaps closed this month

Tips

  • Rotate the deep dive so each measure gets a close look a few times a year.
  • If a number is disputed, assign someone to validate it and move on.
  • Keep decisions in writing, with a name and a date.
  • Add a standing line for upcoming CMS deadlines so none surprise the group.

At a glance

  • Sixty minutes, seven items
  • One rotating deep dive instead of a tour of every measure
  • Every meeting ends with owners and dates
