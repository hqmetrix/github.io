Case study

What to do when a large qualified registry does not meet your needs

A client's registry vendor handled support through tickets. With a deadline near, the client came to us. Work is in progress.

Case Studies · Anonymized · Last checked November 17, 2026

← Case Studies

Problem

The client's registry handled its quality submission. Questions went through a helpdesk ticket queue, answers were slow relative to the submission calendar, and the client could not see how its numbers were produced. They approached us, and we are working with them to solve their data submission problem.

Required Data

  • The data the registry already receives, and what it returns
  • Patient-level source data for each measure
  • The registry's results by practice and measure
  • The submission calendar and internal cutoffs

FHIR Sources

Sources are the same as for any ACO: patient, encounter, condition, observation and procedure data from each practice's systems, via FHIR where available and extracts where not.

QRDA Generation

We calculate measures independently from patient-level data and can produce QRDA III or QPP JSON from it. This does not replace the registry. It gives the client a second set of numbers and a fallback.

Validation

  • Compare independent results with the registry's by practice and measure.
  • Investigate every difference above an agreed threshold.
  • Check completeness against eligible patients.
  • Keep a written record of differences and how each was resolved.

Submission

Who submits is the client's decision. Options include staying with the registry using independent validation, or moving to another registry before the window. A third party can submit for an ACO, and the ACO reviews what was submitted on the QPP site. Confirm authorization steps well before the window, because data cannot be corrected after it closes.

Common Errors

  • Waiting for a ticket answer in the last two weeks
  • Not knowing which number is the registry's and which is your own
  • No agreed escalation path
  • Changing vendors too late to test a file
  • No written record of what was submitted

At a glance

  • Setting: ACO using a large registry vendor
  • Issue: ticket-based support and limited visibility
  • Status: work in progress with the client
  • Details are generalized. No client names, and no results are claimed that are not described here.
