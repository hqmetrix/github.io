Overview

ACOs in the Medicare Shared Savings Program report quality through the Alternative Payment Model Performance Pathway. Quality scores feed directly into shared savings and losses, so data accuracy matters financially, not just for compliance.

hqMetrix helps ACOs collect and validate data from participating practices, understand how each practice contributes to the ACO's score, and focus outreach on the measures that move results.

Who it is for

  • Independent MSSP ACOs
  • ACO quality directors and analytics teams
  • Practice managers inside ACO participant groups

How it works

  1. Onboard practicesCollect data specifications and test extracts from each practice.
  2. NormalizeStandardize inputs into a common measure logic.
  3. Report & reconcileCompare computed results to practice-reported figures and resolve differences.
  4. ImprovePublish gap lists and monthly scorecards to practices.

Common questions

Does this replace our existing analytics vendor?

It can, or it can sit alongside it as a validation layer. Many ACOs start with a validation engagement.

Which program years do you support?

We work to the specifications in force for the program year being reported. Confirm current requirements with CMS before finalizing any plan.
