Overview

The Merit-based Incentive Payment System adjusts Medicare Part B payments based on performance across several categories. Success depends on picking measures that suit your population and capturing clean, complete data for them.

We help you shortlist measures, test them against real data, and monitor them throughout the year. The goal is no surprises at submission time.

Who it is for

  • Small and mid-sized practices
  • Groups with multiple EHRs
  • Organizations that want transparency on how scores are built

Common questions

Yes. The reporting level is chosen during scoping based on what benefits your practice.

What if we find a problem late in the year?

We prioritize the highest-impact fixes and document anything that cannot be corrected for the current year.
