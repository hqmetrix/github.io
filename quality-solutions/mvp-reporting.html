Overview

MIPS Value Pathways group measures and activities around a specialty or condition so that reporting reflects the care a clinician actually delivers. For many practices the hard part is not the submission itself. It is deciding whether an MVP fits, and then proving the data behind each measure.

hqMetrix works with your team to evaluate the available MVPs against your clinician mix and patient panel, map your clinical and claims data to the measures involved, and track progress all year instead of in the final weeks.

Who it is for

  • Independent and multi-specialty practices
  • Group practices moving from traditional MIPS
  • Specialty clinicians seeking a more relevant measure set

How it works

  1. AssessWe review your clinicians, specialties and data sources and recommend a pathway.
  2. Map & validateMeasures are mapped to your data and tested against sample populations.
  3. MonitorDashboards track numerators, denominators and exclusions through the year.
  4. Submit & reviewFinal data is validated, submitted through the agreed pathway, and reviewed with you afterward.

Common questions

Is an MVP right for every practice?

No. Whether an MVP beats traditional MIPS depends on your specialty, patient volume and data. The assessment step answers that with your own numbers.

Do you work with our EHR?

We work from the data you can extract, including EHR exports, QRDA files and claims. See Clinical Data Extraction for details.

How early should we start?

Early in the performance year is ideal, but mid-year starts are common and still useful.
