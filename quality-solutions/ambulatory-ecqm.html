Overview

Electronic clinical quality measures depend on structured data captured during care. When the data is incomplete or mapped incorrectly, results can look worse than the care actually delivered.

We help ambulatory practices and ACO participants test their eCQM output end to end: from the value sets and fields behind each measure to the QRDA files that are ultimately submitted.

Who it is for

  • Practices whose eCQM scores look wrong
  • ACOs consolidating data from several EHRs
  • Quality teams preparing for audits

Common questions

Where do measure specifications come from?

Always from the official eCQI Resource Center for the relevant reporting year. We recommend verifying versions there.

Is this for hospital reporting?

No. This service is for clinician and group (ambulatory) measures only.
