Overview

Many gaps close with a simple conversation: a reminder, a question, a scheduled visit. Care teams rarely have the hours to have that conversation with every patient.

The outreach assistant sends personalized text messages, handles routine replies, and routes anything unusual to a person. Annual Wellness Visit scheduling is the first workflow; others follow the same pattern.

Who it is for

  • Practices and ACOs with large panels
  • Care management teams short on capacity
  • Quality teams measuring AWV completion

Messaging must follow applicable consent and privacy rules. We configure workflows around your organization's compliance requirements.
