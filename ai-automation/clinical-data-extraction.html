Overview

Quality programs depend on discrete clinical data such as lab results, vitals, screenings and diagnoses. Some EHRs expose that data poorly, or generate quality files that do not reflect what is in the chart.

We build repeatable extraction pipelines that read from supported database or export sources, standardize the content, and feed it to measure logic and registry submissions.

Who it is for

  • Practices on EHRs with weak reporting tools
  • ACOs consolidating data across vendors
  • Teams preparing registry submissions
