hqMetrix AI

Generative AI built for quality teams

Quality work is repetitive and deadline-driven: reading validation errors, ranking gap lists, writing reminders, answering the same questions each month. hqMetrix AI takes the first pass so your team spends its time on the patients and fixes that matter.

It works on top of the same dashboards you already trust, so every suggestion traces back to a number you can inspect.

PRIORITIZE

Gap prioritization

Ranks open gaps by closability: visit already booked, recent contact, measure weight and days left in the year, so outreach starts where it pays off.

VALIDATE

QRDA I failure triage

Reads schematron and validation errors, groups them by root cause, names the EHR field or mapping behind them and suggests the fix for the owning team.

ASK

Ask hqMetrix

Ask a question about your measures in plain English and get an answer drawn from your own dashboards, with the numbers and filters it used shown beside it.

OUTREACH

Outreach drafting

Drafts patient and practice messages for each gap type, such as a mammogram reminder or a blood pressure recheck, for your team to review before anything is sent.

DETECT

Chart evidence finder

Scans notes and scanned documents for screenings or results that were done but never coded, and queues them for clinician review as possible gap closures.

FORECAST

Year-end forecast

Projects where each measure will land at year end from the current closure pace, and how many more compliant patients reach the next benchmark band.
