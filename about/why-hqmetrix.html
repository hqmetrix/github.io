From every EHR to one ACO-level file

For the five APP Plus eCQMs (CMS2, CMS122, CMS125, CMS130 and CMS165), this is the path we run with you. We know the formats and timelines involved: CCDA, QRDA, the data submission calendar and the different data collection types.

  1. CollectWe collect QRDA I files from each practice.
  2. CorrectWe find what is broken and correct it with the practice.
  3. MatchWe match and deduplicate patients across TINs.
  4. CalculateWe calculate the measures and show you where you stand.
  5. SubmitWe submit one ACO-level eCQM file.

Why eCQM reporting

eCQM reporting keeps the reporting incentive available, which Medicare CQM reporters cannot earn. Check the current thresholds on the ACO APP reporting page and with CMS.

The calendar is the reason to start now

Care gaps close on December 31, so seeing where your measures stand now is what lets you act on them. Data problems can be fixed up to submission, and that is where we help you make the March 31, 2027 deadline.
