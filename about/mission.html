About hqMetrix

Our mission

Make quality data trustworthy, understandable and useful to the people providing care.

Quality programs generate enormous amounts of data and very little shared understanding. Practices often receive a score without knowing how it was built, and then are asked to improve it.

We believe every number should be explainable. That means documenting logic, showing the underlying records and validating results against official sources.

We aim to be a practical partner for independent practices and ACOs: clear about what we know, honest about what we do not, and focused on results that help patients.

What we stand for

Transparency

Every calculation can be traced and explained.

Accuracy

We validate before we report.

Practicality

Tools should fit real workflows.

Respect for data

PHI is handled with care, always.
