Overview

Quality teams often work from spreadsheets assembled by hand, which are out of date almost as soon as they are sent. hqMetrix dashboards refresh from your data on a defined schedule so everyone works from the same numbers.

Each view drills from organization to practice to clinician to patient, and every figure can be traced back to its source records.

Who it is for

  • Quality and population health directors
  • Practice managers and medical directors
  • Care management teams
