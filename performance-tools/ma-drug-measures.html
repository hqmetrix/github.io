Overview

Part D measures such as medication adherence are driven by pharmacy fills, not by clinic visits. That makes them easy to miss in a clinical quality program, even though they carry significant weight in plan ratings.

We calculate these measures from pharmacy claims, identify members who are falling behind, and give care teams practical lists to act on, such as 90-day supply conversions or outreach.

Who it is for

  • MA plan pharmacy and quality teams
  • Provider groups with delegated care management
  • Pharmacy partners
