Overview

Star Ratings influence quality bonus payments and member choice for Medicare Advantage plans. Dozens of measures contribute, many with different data sources, lags and weights, which makes prioritization difficult.

hqMetrix helps plans and provider groups monitor measure performance during the year, estimate cut-point positions, and direct member outreach toward the gaps with the greatest effect.

Who it is for

  • Medicare Advantage plans
  • Provider groups in MA risk contracts
  • Plan quality and Stars teams

Common questions

Which measures are covered?

Those in the current Star Ratings technical notes. Confirm current measure sets and weights with CMS.

Can provider groups use this without a plan contract?

Groups with plan-supplied data can use it. Scope depends on the data you have.
