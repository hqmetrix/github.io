Overview

Value-based contracts are settled against benchmarks that are published after the performance year ends. Waiting for the final report leaves no time to act. A model that recomputes the same logic from your own claims shows likely outcomes while there is still time to influence them.

The modeler reproduces key benchmark and risk-adjustment logic, validates it against known results, and projects savings or losses under different assumptions.

Who it is for

  • ACO finance and analytics leaders
  • Groups evaluating a move to downside risk
  • Boards that need an early view of financial results

Common questions

How accurate are the projections?

They are estimates. Accuracy depends on data completeness and how closely the model replicates official methodology, which we validate and document.

Can this support stop-loss decisions?

It can provide the exposure analysis that informs those decisions. It does not replace advice from an actuary or insurance broker.
